About

Born in Los Angeles. Pomona College, 1990. UCLA Law, 1995. In practice here ever since.

For years I practiced in Beverly Hills and Century City — where much of Southern California's most demanding legal work gets done — and I've carried that standard into a practice built for individuals and small business.

The work is practical and direct: I take the matter you bring me, tell you plainly where you stand, and handle it myself — no layers, no handoffs, no junior associate you've never met. That's why clients come with one problem —

“and usually stay.”
